1. Executive Summary
Product Name

Prime Wave

Product Vision

Prime Wave is an AI-powered Software Engineering Learning, Assessment, Certification, and Hiring Platform that transforms students into industry-ready software professionals through structured semester-based learning, AI mentorship, real-world projects, automated evaluation, recruiter verification, and direct hiring.

Unlike traditional EdTech platforms that focus only on learning, Prime Wave creates a complete ecosystem connecting students, universities, AI mentors, recruiters, HR teams, and companies.

2. Business Problem

Current problems include:

Students learn theory but lack practical skills.
Certificates often do not reflect real abilities.
Recruiters spend significant time screening candidates.
Universities have limited visibility into students' industry readiness.
Companies struggle to identify job-ready freshers.
Students build portfolios without standardized evaluation.

Prime Wave addresses these issues by introducing AI-driven learning, validation, certification, and hiring.

3. Business Objectives
Build an AI Software Engineering Academy.
Deliver industry-level practical education.
Evaluate every learning activity using AI.
Generate AI-verified portfolios.
Create a standardized Professional Score.
Enable recruiters to hire based on verified skills.
Reduce hiring time.
Improve placement success.
Increase student employability.
